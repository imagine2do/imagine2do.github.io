English

Privacy at a glance: Photos, captions, and optional highlight audio stay on your device and are not sent to us or the advertising SDK. NoBooth has no account system or developer-operated photo server. Optional rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads and UMP for advertising and consent data. NoBooth Plus provides its benefits without ads.

1. Scope and contact

This policy explains how NoBooth handles information when you use the mobile app and its support pages. For privacy questions, contact hipdo.dev@gmail.com.

2. Information handled on your device

NoBooth uses the following information only to provide app features on your device:

  • photos captured during a four-photo session;
  • video and optional ambient audio used to create a highlight;
  • captions, selected frame, tone, layout, timing, and session date;
  • past-session files and metadata stored in the app's private storage; and
  • device language settings used to select Korean, English, or Japanese.

This information is not uploaded to a server operated by us and is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, facial recognition, or AI training.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take photos or record a highlight.
  • Microphone: used only when you enable sound for a highlight. If you deny access, NoBooth can create a silent highlight.
  • Add to Photos/Gallery: used when you choose to save a finished photo strip or highlight. NoBooth does not request broad access to read your photo library.

You can change permissions in your device settings. NoBooth does not access the camera or microphone in the background.

4. NoBooth Plus purchases

NoBooth Plus is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes your store account, payment method, transaction, and purchase history under its own privacy policy. NoBooth reads product and entitlement information from the store to display a local price, unlock Plus, and restore a purchase. We do not receive your card or bank details and do not operate a separate receipt server.

5. Saving, sharing, and printing

Saving to Photos/Gallery, sharing, and printing occur only after you choose the relevant action. Content then goes to the destination you select, such as the system photo library, share recipient, app, printer, or print service. That destination handles the content under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not receive a copy.

6. Information we may receive outside the app

  • Support email: if you contact us, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you voluntarily provide. We use them only to respond and troubleshoot.
  • Support website: these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web data such as IP address, browser/device information, request time, and pages viewed under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add cookies, analytics, or advertising to these pages.

7. Optional ads and third-party data processing

The first highlight save is free. After that, you may choose to complete a rewarded ad for one additional highlight save with the NoBooth mark. Ads are optional; capture and free photo saving do not require watching an ad. Plus users can save unlimited highlights on supported devices without ads.

Google Mobile Ads may process IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Google UMP manages privacy choices and related consent information. We request non-personalized ads and do not request Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing; the specific information depends on the device, settings, and consent choices. We do not send photos, videos, audio, or captions to these SDKs.

See the Google Privacy Policy, iOS advertising data disclosure, and Android advertising data disclosure. Apple or Google also processes store purchases, Google handles support email, and GitHub hosts the support website. We may disclose support records when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Past sessions remain in the app's private storage until you delete each session in NoBooth or uninstall the app.
  • Copies you export to Photos/Gallery, another app, or a print service remain there until you delete them from that destination.
  • Purchase records are retained by Apple or Google according to their policies and legal obligations.
  • Google handles the retention and deletion of advertising and consent data under its policies. Deleting local photo sessions does not delete data already processed by Google. See Google advertising choices and contact us with privacy requests.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, normally no longer than one year after resolution, unless a longer period is required by law.

9. Security

Keeping photo-session data on the device limits external exposure. NoBooth relies on the operating system's app sandbox and permission controls. Store transactions use Apple or Google systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and store account.

10. Children

NoBooth is not directed to children under 13 and does not provide accounts or social features. Its ad requests are non-personalized. If you believe a child sent personal information through support email or have a concern about data processing, contact us so we can review the request.

11. International processing and your choices

Apple, Google, GitHub, or their providers may process store, advertising, consent, email, or website data in countries other than your own under their privacy policies. Where required, the app presents a privacy form before requesting ads and provides ad privacy settings to revisit your choices. Availability of ads can depend on those choices and your region. You can continue using free capture and photo saving without choosing a rewarded ad, or use Plus without ads.

You can also deny optional device permissions, create highlights without audio, delete local sessions, remove exported content from its destination, uninstall NoBooth, or contact us about support information you provided.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when NoBooth, applicable law, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when revisions are posted.

첫 하이라이트 저장은 무료입니다. 이후에는 보상 광고를 직접 선택해 끝까지 시청하면 NoBooth 마크가 포함된 하이라이트를 1회 추가 저장할 수 있습니다. 광고 시청은 선택 사항이며, 촬영과 무료 사진 저장에는 광고 시청이 필요하지 않습니다. Plus 이용자는 지원 기기에서 광고 없이 하이라이트를 무제한 저장할 수 있습니다.

Google Mobile Ads는 광고 제공, 성과 측정 및 부정 사용 방지를 위해 IP 주소(대략적인 위치를 추정할 수 있음), 기기·앱 식별자, 광고 노출·상호작용, 진단·성능 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다. Google UMP는 개인정보 관련 선택과 동의 정보를 관리합니다. 앱은 비개인화 광고를 요청하며 Apple의 추적 권한을 요청하지 않습니다. 비개인화 광고도 데이터 처리를 포함하며, 구체적인 처리 정보는 기기·설정·동의 선택에 따라 달라집니다. 사진, 영상, 음성 또는 문구는 이 SDK에 보내지 않습니다.

Google 개인정보처리방침, iOS 광고 데이터 공개 안내, Android 광고 데이터 공개 안내를 참고해 주세요. 스토어 구매는 Apple 또는 Google, 지원 이메일은 Google, 지원 웹사이트 호스팅은 GitHub가 처리합니다. 관련 법령이나 적법한 절차에 따라 요구되는 경우 지원 기록을 제공할 수 있습니다.

8. 保持と削除

  • 過去のセッションは、NoBooth内で個別に削除するか、アプリをアンインストールするまで、アプリ専用ストレージに残ります。
  • 写真／ギャラリー、別のアプリ、印刷サービスへ書き出したコピーは、その宛先で利用者が削除するまで残る場合があります。
  • 購入記録はAppleまたはGoogleのポリシーおよび法的義務に従って保持されます。
  • 広告・同意データの保持と削除はGoogleのポリシーに従います。端末の撮影履歴を削除しても、Googleがすでに処理したデータは同時に削除されません。Googleの広告に関する選択肢をご確認いただくか、プライバシーについてお問い合わせください。
  • サポートメールは問い合わせ解決に合理的に必要な期間のみ保持し、法令上より長い期間が必要な場合を除き、通常は解決後1年以内に削除します。

9. セキュリティ

撮影データを端末内に保つことで、外部への露出を抑えています。NoBoothはOSのアプリサンドボックスと権限制御を利用し、ストア取引にはAppleまたはGoogleのシステムを使用します。完全に安全な保存・送信方法は存在しないため、端末とストアアカウントを適切に保護してください。

10. 子どものプライバシー

NoBoothは13歳未満の子どもを対象に設計したアプリではなく、アカウントやソーシャル機能を提供しません。広告リクエストは非パーソナライズに限定します。子どもがサポートメールで個人情報を送信したと思われる場合や、データ処理について懸念がある場合は、確認のためお問い合わせください。

11. 国際的な処理と選択肢

Apple、Google、GitHubまたはそのサービス提供者は、それぞれのプライバシーポリシーに基づき、ストア・広告・同意・メール・ウェブサイトの情報を利用者の居住国外で処理する場合があります。必要な地域では広告リクエスト前にプライバシーフォームを表示し、広告のプライバシー設定から選択を変更できます。地域や選択によっては広告を利用できない場合があります。リワード広告を選ばずに無料の撮影と写真保存を続けるか、Plusを広告なしで利用できます。

任意の端末権限を拒否する、無音のハイライトを作成する、撮影履歴や書き出したコンテンツを削除する、NoBoothをアンインストールする、またはサポートで提供した情報について当方に連絡することもできます。
