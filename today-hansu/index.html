A few useful answers

Where are my photos stored?

Your photos and stories are processed on your device. We do not upload them to a server. My catches keeps the latest 12 records; save important photos separately.

Can I use Hansu for free?

Yes. Newspaper, magazine, award, receipt, and postcard designs are free. Three special designs can be unlocked for one photo by earning an optional ad reward in the iOS or Android app.

What if an ad is unavailable?

Keep using any free design, or try again later. Closing an ad before earning the reward does not unlock special designs. Saved earned rewards stay with the photo’s record.
