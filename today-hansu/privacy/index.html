Hansu (오늘의 한 수) is provided by Hipdo. Contact: hipdo.dev@gmail.com.

Photos and stories

We create your designs on your device using the photo and words you choose. We do not upload them to a server or require an account or location permission.

Local records and deletion

My catches keeps your latest 12 records. Use Delete to remove a record. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data may erase records. Save important photos separately.

Exported images

New PNG images do not copy the original photo’s location or EXIF metadata. Anything visible in the photo, including scenery or text, remains visible.

Sharing and dictation

Sharing uses the other app you choose. Keyboard dictation is handled under your keyboard and operating system’s privacy practices.

Optional advertising

On iOS and Android, you may watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad to unlock special designs. Google’s ads SDK may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Photos and stories are not included in ad requests.

Privacy choices

We request non-personalized ads. Where required, Google’s privacy choices appear first. You can review or change applicable choices under Help & settings → Ad privacy choices. Free designs remain available without watching an ad. The web version does not request ads.

Rewards and example photos

An earned design unlock is stored on this device with that photo’s record. Deleting the record removes the unlock. There are no payments or generative AI servers. The example photo is an AI-generated illustration.

Support requests and this website

If you email us, we use the message and your email address to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive photos or passwords. This support website has no advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider processes network requests, including IP addresses, to serve and protect the website. Ads in the mobile app are governed by Google’s linked privacy information, including retention and international processing.

Requests and choices

For questions or applicable access, correction, deletion, or objection requests about information you send us, contact hipdo.dev@gmail.com. Photos and records are stored locally, so you control their deletion on your device. Where applicable, you may also contact your local data protection authority. We update this page when our practices change.
