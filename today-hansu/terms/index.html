USING HANSU

A keepsake, made your way.

Use photos you have the right to use. Headlines are suggested from your selected moment and words, and you can edit them. The app does not identify or certify species, measurements, or locations.

Newspaper, magazine, award, receipt, and postcard designs are free to save and share. In the iOS or Android app, earning one ad reward unlocks three special designs for that photo. Closing an ad before earning the reward does not unlock them; free designs remain available.

If no ad is available or you are offline, use a free design or try later. Earned special designs remain available in the saved record. Choosing a new photo requires a new reward. There are no purchases or subscriptions.

Records stay on this device. Save important photos separately.

Personal designs are playful photo templates, not news reporting, fishing records certification, or promises of ad availability. The example photo is AI-generated. Contact Hipdo at hipdo.dev@gmail.com for help.
